FAQ

Is this an adults-only event?

Yes, we love your little ones, but we're asking that this celebration be an adults-only affair. Thank you for understanding — we hope this gives you a night off to relax and celebrate with us.

What should I wear?

Semi-formal, garden party style — think greens, yellows and blues. Please avoid white, orange and pink. See the full dress code on the Event Details page.

Is there parking on-site?

Yes! Free on-site parking is available, and a staff member will guide you to your spot when you arrive.

What happens if it rains?

We'll celebrate rain or shine! If the weather changes any plans, we'll post an update here.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We can only accommodate the guests named on your invitation. When you RSVP, please add the name of each invited guest.

Will there be dancing?

Absolutely! The reception & party starts at 5:30 PM — bring your dancing shoes.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 3:00 PM so you're settled in before the ceremony begins at 3:30 PM.

Who do I contact with questions?

Reach out to Heloisa or Rebeca directly — we're happy to help!
